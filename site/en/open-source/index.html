Contributing and the CLA

Contributions from third parties are accepted under the CLA. It allows the steward, Schulverwalter GmbH, to distribute the apps in app stores; the public version stays GPL-3.0-or-later. The CLA text is a draft that still needs a lawyer's review. See CONTRIBUTING.md and the Code of Conduct.

What we deliberately do not do

  • No extraction of keys or certificates, no FairPlay, AirServer or AirParrot material
  • No DRM circumvention
  • No Cast receiving in v1, no AirDrop, no DLNA/UPnP, no Miracast sender
