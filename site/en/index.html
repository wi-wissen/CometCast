One app. Sender and receiver.

Comet Cast sends your screen, documents, links and media to Apple TV, Google TV / Android TV, mini PCs and browsers. It is meant for schools, offices and clubs, where many different devices meet in one room.

What Comet Cast is

One app for both sides

The same app sends and receives. The idea is similar to LocalSend, but for presenting on a display.

As few installs as possible

The side with fewer devices gets the app. The other side uses a browser or a system feature it already has.

Many kinds of content

Screen, PDF documents, images, links and media. The receiver has a toolbar (pen, marker, laser, freeze, black screen).

Where you can send to

  • Browsers (sender and receiver, no install)
  • Mini PCs and desktops (Linux, Windows, macOS)
  • Android TV / Google TV
  • Apple TV (native receiver)

Not all of this runs yet. The status per platform is in the table.

Connecting with a room code

The receiver shows a room code (or QR code) and the sender enters it. Then the devices connect directly if the network allows it. More.
