Works. 2 ways, the easiest first:

    Also works across networks

  2. On your deviceComet Cast app
    On the screen · Apple TVNothing to install

    Also works across networks

    Photos and videos right away; your screen with a short delay.

    On the screen · Google TVComet Cast app on it

    Also works across networks

Then it is three steps

  1. The screen shows a code

    And a QR code, and the address to open.

  2. You enter it or scan it

    Or tap the screen in the app's list of screens nearby.

  3. You present

    Your screen, a PDF, a photo or a link. Need a moment? Press pause and the room keeps the last picture.
